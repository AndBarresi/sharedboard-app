SharedBoard.app

Your ideas and your existing AI assistant, on the same local canvas.

Windows x64 · Portable ZIP

Start in three steps

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder.
  2. Double-click Start SharedBoard.app.cmd. Your browser opens the board.
  3. Choose Connect an AI on the board and follow the generated local setup.

The Node runtime and drawing assets are included. You do not need to install developer tools to open the board. Your AI client must already be installed and signed in.

Your work stays with your copy

A new data folder appears on first launch. It holds this copy's drawings, history, messages and private connection files. Every tester starts with an empty board and their own identity. Use Open data folder.cmd to find it.

Wait for Saved on this computer, then double-click Stop SharedBoard.app.cmd to stop your copy. Closing the browser tab alone leaves the local service available to your assistant.

Share the original ZIP

Send the original release archive to testers. A used data folder contains personal work and credentials; keep it private. Keep your extraction in place after pairing, because AI settings use its exact local paths.

Help shape the next version

Test a saved drawing, an AI discussion, and a small requested drawing change. Record friction in FEEDBACK.txt. There is no automatic telemetry or feedback upload.
